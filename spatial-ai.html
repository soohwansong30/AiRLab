Spatial AI

We work on visual localization, 3D reconstruction, scanning, and scene understanding, enabling robots to build and continually update accurate 3D representations of their surroundings.

Visual SLAM

  • Localization: robustly extracts dense correspondences under extreme conditions — lighting change, repetitive patterns, textureless regions — for precise camera pose estimation.
  • Mapping: real-time, high-precision 3D reconstruction via online multi-view stereo.
2 top-tier AI conference papers (AAAI-23, CVPR-24) · 1 top journal, IEEE TIP (top 2% JCR)

Image-based real-time, high-precision camera localization and 3D mapping technology.

  • TopicFM: Robust and Interpretable Topic-Assisted Feature Matching — AAAI 2023
  • Learning to Produce Semi-Dense Correspondences for Visual Localization — CVPR 2024
  • TopicFM+: Boosting Accuracy and Efficiency of Topic-Assisted Feature Matching — IEEE TIP, 2024

MVS & Neural Rendering

  • Multi-view stereo (MVS): high-precision 3D reconstruction using a depth foundation model, ranking 6th on the Tanks and Temples benchmark.
  • Online 3DGS reconstruction: generates and updates a 3D Gaussian map in real time for photorealistic, high-quality rendering.
  • Large-scale urban reconstruction: rapidly reconstructs wide urban areas in 3D, using a video diffusion model to synthesize novel-view images and fill in data-sparse regions.
3 top-tier conference papers (ICLR-22, IJCAI-25, CVPR-26) · 1 top journal, Pattern Recognition (top 8.8%) · 3 SCIE papers · 3 technology transfers

3D reconstruction technology based on multi-view stereo (MVS) and 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS).

  • Curvature-Guided Dynamic Scale Networks for Multi-View Stereo — ICLR 2022
  • Online 3D Gaussian Splatting Modeling with Novel View Selection — IJCAI 2025
  • PR-IQA: Partial-Reference Image Quality Assessment for Diffusion-Based Novel View Synthesis — CVPR 2026
  • Prior Depth-Based Multi-View Stereo Network for Online 3D Model Reconstruction — Pattern Recognition, 2023

3D Scanning

Performs real-time 3D reconstruction from drone aerial imagery (online MVS), instantly identifies weakly-reconstructed regions, and plans an optimal re-scanning path — enabling complete 3D scanning of large-scale structures in a single flight.

4 top-tier robotics conference papers (ICRA-17/18/20, IROS-26) · 1 top journal, IEEE T-RO (top 7.3%) · 4 SCIE papers

Optimal 3D scanning path-planning technology.

  • Active 3D Modeling via Online Multi-View Stereo — ICRA 2020
  • Online Coverage and Inspection Planning for 3D Modeling — Autonomous Robots, 2020
  • View Path Planning via Online Multi-View Stereo for 3D Modeling of Large-Scale Structures — IEEE T-RO, 2022
  • Online Explore-Then-Exploit UAV Scanning with Budgeted Return Re-Scanning — IROS 2026

3D Scene Graphs

We propose a feed-forward framework that combines LLM-generated spatial constraints with best-view visual confidence to build verified 3D scene graphs consistent with the real environment — filtering false-positive objects without costly iterative updates, and generating reliable subtask sequences from verified objects only.

Spatial-constraint-based 3D scene graph and task-planning technology.
