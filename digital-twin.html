Robot Digital Twin

We build standardized, automated pipelines that turn real robots and environments into accurate digital-twin simulations.

Robot Digital Twin Generation

  • Layered information-model framework: a digital-twin simulation standardization architecture that goes beyond single-sensor-driven models, emphasizing URDF-based structural specification.
  • Automatic digital-twin environment construction: infers objects' joint information and physical properties from video data to automatically convert them into simulation environments in real time.
Applied to the National Robot Test Field digital-twin environment construction project

Real-to-Sim robot digital-twin model generation technology.
